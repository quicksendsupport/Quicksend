Three steps, about five minutes.

Everything happens inside WhatsApp Web, in a panel on the right side of the screen.

  1. Choose who to send to

    Open your Excel sheet, paste a list of numbers, or tick people from your saved contacts and groups.

  2. Write the message once

    Pick a ready template or type your own. Click a column name to insert it, like {Name} or {Amount}, and preview the first message.

  3. Send and get a report

    QuickSend sends one by one with a short random pause, and gives you an Excel report of who received it.

Questions people ask.

Does it work on my phone?

QuickSend runs in Chrome on a laptop or desktop with WhatsApp Web. The messages go from your own WhatsApp, so they appear on your phone as usual.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Link your WhatsApp Business app to WhatsApp Web the same way and use QuickSend there.

Will my number get blocked?

QuickSend sends from your own WhatsApp Web with a random pause between messages, like you would. Message people who know you, personalise each message and start with smaller lists. If WhatsApp ever shows a warning, pause for a day.

Do you see my customers or messages?

No. Your Excel files, contacts and messages stay in your browser. For Pro, only your license key is checked with our server once a day.

I paid. Where is my license key?

It reaches the email you entered at payment within about 10 minutes. Check Spam and Promotions. Still missing? Write to us with your payment ID.

How do I cancel Pro?

There's nothing to cancel. Pro is prepaid and never charges automatically - it simply ends on the date shown in the panel unless you renew.

Can I send images or PDFs?

Not yet. QuickSend sends text messages with formatting (bold, italic) and emojis.
